Protecting Youth Aging Out of Foster Care in Tennessee

Providing essential training, therapeutic animal care, and military mentorship to protect vulnerable youth before they age out of the system.

Join Us in Changing Lives Learn More

A Unique Approach to Saving Lives

Combining therapeutic animal care with military mentorship for at-risk youth.

Therapeutic Animal Interaction

Working with rescued feral cats teaches empathy, responsibility, and patience. Youth learn to build trust, provide care, and understand the value of compassion.

  • Develops emotional intelligence and empathy
  • Builds confidence through meaningful contribution
  • Creates a calming, therapeutic environment

Military & Law Enforcement Mentorship

One-on-one mentorship from experienced veterans who understand service, sacrifice, and character building, preparing youth for military readiness and life success.

  • Experienced veteran mentorship
  • Military readiness and career preparation
  • Life skills training and character development

How The Program Works

01

Initial Assessment

Youth are welcomed into a safe environment where we assess their needs, goals, and readiness.

02

Cat Rescue Training

Participants work directly with feral cats, learning empathy, patience, and animal care.

03

Mentor Pairing

Matched with a retired military or law enforcement mentor for one-on-one guidance.

04

Life Skills Training

Intensive training in discipline, fitness, leadership, and practical life skills.

05

Military Preparation

Comprehensive preparation for military entrance exams and service requirements.

06

Launch to Success

Graduates leave equipped with confidence, skills, and ongoing support to thrive.
